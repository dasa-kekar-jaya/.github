## About Us

PT. Dasa Kekar Jaya is a facade contractor based in Bekasi, Indonesia.

Since 2018, we have worked on aluminum, glass, curtain wall, and Aluminium Composite Panel projects. Our work covers planning, material preparation, production, and installation based on the needs of each project.

## What We Do

We provide facade systems for commercial, industrial, and residential buildings.

Our services include Curtain Wall Glass, Frameless Glass, Aluminium Window Wall, Automatic Glass, Railing Glass, Spider Glass System, Aluminium Composite Panel, Doors and Windows, and Skylight.

## About This Organization

This GitHub organization is used to store and manage our company website, source code, documentation, and other digital projects.

It also keeps our company projects in one place so they can be maintained and continued by the team over time.
